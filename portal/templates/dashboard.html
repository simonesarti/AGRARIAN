Flying a slot

  1. Type the slot's ingest URL into the drone controller's RTMP settings.
  2. Start streaming. Processing starts on its own — nothing to press here.
  3. The slot turns Live and a Watch button appears.

Lost a key, or typed it somewhere you should not have? New key replaces it immediately; a flight already in the air is unaffected and keeps running until it lands.
